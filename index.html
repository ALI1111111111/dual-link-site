Dual Link

Texts and calls from your SIM phone, on your other phone. Over your own Wi-Fi, end-to-end encrypted, no servers.

How it works

  • Install Dual Link on both phones and scan one QR code to pair them.
  • New texts and incoming calls on the SIM phone show up on your other phone in real time.
  • Reply from your other phone; the SMS is sent from your SIM, with your usual number.
