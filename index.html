You've probably been here.

It happens to anyone with two phones, a work SIM, a new phone and an old number, or a phone that just stays on the charger.

"Your code has expired."

You're paying online. The OTP lands on the phone upstairs. By the time you're back, the payment has timed out.

Three missed calls.

Hours later you see them. Someone needed you, and you never knew the phone rang.

Two phones, all day.

You check one, then the other, then the first one again, just in case.

Why the usual fixes don't work: a SIM can only sit in one phone at a time. Call forwarding moves calls but not texts. And banks and carriers won't forward OTP codes.

How it works

Two minutes to set up. After that, you forget it's there.

  1. Install on both phones

    The phone with your SIM, and the one you actually carry. Both on the same Wi-Fi.

  2. Scan one QR code

    The SIM phone shows a code. Scan it with the other phone. No account, no sign-up.

  3. Leave the SIM phone home

    Texts, codes and calls show up in your hand in about a second. Reply from your real number.

Your messages never leave your phones.

  • No server in the middleThe two phones talk directly over your Wi-Fi.
  • End-to-end encryptedAES-256, with a key made on your phone when you pair.
  • Nothing to sign up forNo account, no ads, no tracking.
  • Open sourceAnyone can read the code on GitHub.

Questions people ask

Is it safe to send my OTP codes this way?

Your codes go straight from your SIM phone to your other phone over your own Wi-Fi, encrypted with a key that only those two phones have. There's no server that could see or store them.

Do both phones need internet?

No internet needed. Both phones just need to be on the same Wi-Fi network, like your home or office Wi-Fi. Syncing across different networks is on the roadmap.

Can I answer calls on the other phone?

Calls ring on the phone in your hand with the caller's name. You can decline, reply with a text, or end the call, and see your call history. The call itself is still answered on the SIM phone.

Does it drain the battery?

Barely. When nothing is happening, the connection uses under 1% of the CPU. Keep the SIM phone on the charger and you won't notice it.

Does it work with iPhone?

Not yet. iPhone support, using an iPhone as the phone in your hand, is coming next.

Is it free?

Yes. Dual Link is free and open source under the GPL-3.0 license.
