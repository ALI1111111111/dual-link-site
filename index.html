How it works

1. Pair once

Install on both phones and scan one QR code. No accounts.

2. Texts arrive instantly

New SMS on Phone A appear on Phone B in about a second, with one-tap code copy.

3. Reply with your number

Replies go out from Phone A's SIM. Pick the SIM on dual-SIM phones.

4. Calls ring on Phone B

See who's calling, decline or reply by SMS, and check call history.

Private by design

Phone to phone

No tracking

No accounts, no ads, no analytics. Read the code on GitHub.

Reliable
